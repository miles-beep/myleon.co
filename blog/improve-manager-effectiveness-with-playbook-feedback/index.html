Improve manager effectiveness with Playbook Feedback

Making significant decisions on how we lead teams of people is a lot like being on a diving board, alone and afraid to jump.
Playbook Articles
Share this:




It can be lonely, and truth be told, driven by cognitive bias, poor feedback strategies, and one-sided heuristics. Yet, every decision we make regarding how we drive performance within our teams comes from a lineage of learned processes you either saw previously in your professional career or read in countless "thought leader" blogs bombarded on your daily LinkedIn feed.

Most if not all the decisions we make - no matter how big or small - lack a decision-making framework to better understand if this is the right decision, at the right time, for the right team.

And most importantly, they lack a feedback loop to help you understand the sentiment around the decision.

For example, want to set up a team happy hour for that burned-out team at your nearest watering hole?

How do you do it now?

The motivated leader you are, goes ahead, get's budget approval if needed, find's the place, maybe looks at that shared calendar, and boom, it gets posted publicly out on Slack.

And what happens? 

You get half-ass feedback, maybe some cool little fire emojis, and comments by your extroverted group of employees who are ready to get "lit."

But what about that under-served, maybe a little introverted group of employees? What about that burned-out, tired of your face, group of employees who need time away from work and understand that a couple of "car-bombs" and bar wings won't solve the world's problems.

Yes, I understand this is hyperbole. 

But that doesn't change the fact that we improve ourselves and our teams through feedback on our decisions as managers.

From team happy hours to starting a 4-day-work-week, every decision we make should involve feedback—real-time learning, which can act as validations tools for our choices.

And most importantly, we need feedback to act as a proxy for your employees to understand that their thoughts and feelings matter.

How does Playbook feedback work?

The first step in using real-time feedback to drive decision-making is part of what we call "Wellness Intelligence." 

This process involves using LEON Check-ins to understand team sentiment and readiness to perform. And, of course, the Playbook Library with over 200 tried-and-true strategies designed to super-power your team.

Once you decide on the best Playbook for your team today, you will want to customize your Playbook with notes to your team and send it away. 

From there, within the Employee Dashboard, your team will be able to:
