Find and hire a LEON Expert today.

There's a new resource to find LEON performance managers, mental health experts, and well-being leaders for your company: LEON Experts.

Congratulations! You are about to start using LEON and have decided you need some extra help. The LEON Experts network is a fantastic resource to help you kick-start your managers and company using LEON.

Before you rush off to hit send on working with an expert, I want to give you a brief overview of what a LEON Expert is and how they can super-power your LEON Platform.

‍

Who are these so-called LEON Experts?

LEON Experts are performance managers, mental health experts, and well-being leaders who provide services to SMB, mid-market, and enterprise customers using the LEON Platform.

Over the past year, we've been recruiting a team of Experts to be a part of our Expert team:

  • Reaching out to elite performance coaches.
  • Diving deep into what makes a great LEON Expert.
  • And trying to understand the future of work and what that means for today's company.

‍

‍

Some of these Experts have been with LEON since the beginning. Others picked it up recently. But all of them caught our attention with top-quality content, proven industry experience, and an aligned vision of how LEON can support teams and companies of all sizes.

From world-class Ph.D. sports scientists to previous sales leaders who now improve mental health in fast scaling revenue teams, we built our team of Experts to help any size company super-power LEON within their company.

So if you're a company looking for a fractional chief well-being officer, a VP of Sales in need of a performance coach, or just looking to learn how to super-charge your LEON platform, you know where to go.

‍

The world is full of talented, educated, and motivated wellbeing leaders, and we couldn't be more excited to showcase individuals to LEON clients and employee members.

- Bryan Smith, COO and Founder of LEON

‍

How does it work?

LEON Experts are a resource for both LEON users and non-LEON users.

  • Are you looking for a mental health coach to come to help your struggling sales team? Then, a LEON Expert can help.
  • What about a burnout expert to support your HR team in designing a robust and inclusive well-being benefits package? No problem.
  • Or maybe your goals are a bit more aggressive, and you need that Wendy Rhodes type from Billions to help scale team performance to the moon? Then, we got you, Bobby Axelrod!

Either way, LEON Experts provide personalized team coaching to help supercharge your team performance with individualized training, data analysis, and Playbook recommendations for HR leadership and front-line managers.

Getting started with a LEON Expert

Once you’ve selected a LEON Expert, you get to move onto the most exciting part — getting started.

Why do all of this work, sign up for LEON, and not hit the ground running? The goal is to create happier, healthier and higher performing teams— not just find “check the well-being box”, right? It’s a little played out, but true: You define the success of LEON.

LEON is a powerful platform and the LEON Experts network is an amazing resource, but without a clearly defined sense of what success is, you could spend time and money running around until you find it. Go check out the LEON Expert site today.
