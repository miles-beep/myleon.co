Happy Holidays Ya Filthy Animals

From our team to yours, we wish you a Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year.

‍

With 2020 in the rear view mirror, we wanted to take a moment to thank you for leaning into change and making your people a priority.

Seriously, our team feels incredibly blessed to be working with so many amazing providers and clients. Together, we got through some stressful sh*t this year and that makes us homies for life.​

Now, before you ride off into the proverbial sunset that is Christmas break, we’d like to send you away with a seasonal jingle that commemorates another holiday season gone by. It goes something like this:

​

’Twas the night before Q4, when all through the house,

​

not a provider was stirring, not even a mouse.

​

The numbers were crunched by the chimney with care,

​

in hopes that HR gurus would soon start to care.


The clients were nestled all snug in their beds,


while visions of LEON danced in their heads.


And Mama in her ‘kerchief, and I in my hat,


had just closed some deals to add to our cap.


When out in the market there arose such a clatter,


I sprang from my bed to check out the chatter.


Away to my desktop I flew like a flash,


turned on the damn thing, scared it might crash.


The light from the screen about blinded me so,


I let out a yelp as I stubbed my big toe,


when, what to my wondering eyes should appear,


but a message from Bryan about to cause cheer.


With a bit of candor, so lively and queer,


I knew in a moment, his emails were clear.

​

More rapid than ever, he spelled it in red,
